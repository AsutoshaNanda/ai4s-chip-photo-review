## Evidence levels

- **Direct local measurement:** file checks, metadata counts, saved predictions, or recorded timings, with artifacts.
- **Primary-source fact:** a rule, provider description, or maintainer statement we read.
- **Published result:** another team's measured finding; not independently reproduced by us.
- **Indirect evidence:** a result from a different task, dataset, modality, or evaluation setting.
- **Assumption/proposal:** a choice such as workload fraction, compute cap, or expected benefit that still needs justification or testing.

Never upgrade one level into another by changing its label. A filename-prefix split remains a proxy until the prefix's meaning is confirmed.
