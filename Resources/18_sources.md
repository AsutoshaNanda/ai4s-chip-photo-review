## Evidence rules

- Being able to read a source, downloading its files, inspecting its code, running its code, repeating its result, and confirming it independently are different stages.
- A participant's saved output is a result they reported, not a measurement we made.
- A model score needs saved predictions, a fixed scoring method, and named test data. No new model scores were created here.
- Proposed dates, priorities, cutoff values, and choices about how to build the project are decisions to test. They are not confirmed results or expected improvements.
- Leave unknowns blank or clearly marked as unresolved. Record how evidence changes a decision.
