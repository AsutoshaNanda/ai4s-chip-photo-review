# What to read

The learning list below is kept from 26 September. The study times are suggestions; they do not assume how much time you have. For the 2 October start, first use [resource readiness](19_Resource_Readiness.md) to find the exact source needed by the next experiment that is ready. Chip and nucleus work can continue while you learn.

| Order | Read | Learn enough to do this | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Official overview](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/ai-4-s-open-innovation-artificial-intelligence-for-life-scien/overview) and [host clarification](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/ai-4-s-open-innovation-artificial-intelligence-for-life-scien/discussion/737840) | Explain the required project and submission | 20 min |
| 2 | [BBBC039 provider page](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC039) | Explain an image, a reference mask that marks the correct objects, and how examples are divided for training and testing | 20 min |
| 3 | [Introduction to Bioimage Analysis](https://bioimagebook.github.io/) | Understand image dots called pixels, brightness, cutoff values, and object measurements | 60 min |
| 4 | [CellProfiler examples](https://cellprofiler.org/examples) | Follow a cell-measurement workflow | 30 min |
| 5 | [Watershed example](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/auto_examples/segmentation/plot_watershed.html) | Separate touching objects | 30 min |
| 6 | [PyTorch basics](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/intro.html) | Load data, train, save, and reload a small model | As needed |

**Free learning option:** [ZeroCostDL4Mic](https://henriqueslab.org/pages/zerocostdl4mic) provides microscopy notebooks and guides using Colab. A notebook combines code, text, and outputs. Check the software each one needs and its data/model usage rights.

## Optional, after the first working result

- [UNet4Nuclei](https://github.com/carpenterlab/unet4nuclei/): research code linked by BBBC039. Useful for understanding how its results are tested; its older software setup may need updating.
- [DSB second-place explanation](https://github.com/jacobkie/2018DSB): why teaching the model to separate individual nuclei matters.
- [RxRx1 explanation](https://www.rxrx.ai/rxrx1): how lab conditions can mislead a model.

Do not spend the first week reading whole courses. Open an image and produce a measured result alongside the learning.
