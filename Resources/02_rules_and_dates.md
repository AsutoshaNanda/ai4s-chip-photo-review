## Required actions

- Complete the [separate registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdRAat5jIunRaFNh_NntsVeJUnekEJDrbuokLZ32LFgCwPtiA/viewform?usp=publish-editor). Joining on Kaggle alone is not enough.
- Have 1 to 5 members and one leader. Each person joins only one team in this challenge.
- Declare one submission category at the start of the Writeup.
- Give credit for outside code, data, models, AI tools, and earlier work. Explain what we added.
- Use resources that we have the right to use. Do not invent results or privately share the main parts of a solution with other teams.
- Provide code that reviewers can run without paid services, special hardware owned or controlled by a particular company, or data that is not public.

## Models with private code, online AI services, and paid computers

Checked live on **3 October 2026**. Section 4 of the [rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/ai-4-s-open-innovation-artificial-intelligence-for-life-scien/rules) clearly allows commercial models, APIs (services that code can call), rented cloud computers, models already trained on other data, and AI tools, as long as their terms are followed. It does not require every model to have publicly available code. Important parts made by others must be listed by name, source, and purpose. Work made with AI help must be checked independently.

The [submission requirements](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/ai-4-s-open-innovation-artificial-intelligence-for-life-scien/overview#submission-requirements) also require public code that reviewers can run to get comparable results without paid services, special company-controlled hardware, or data that is not public. When outside APIs or commercial services are used, they ask for the settings, limits, and other ways to run the project.

**What this means in practice:** Paying for computers to train models, or using models with private code to help build the project, is not banned in all cases. But a submitted system that works only through a paid API conflicts with the requirement that reviewers can repeat the work for free, unless it provides a way to do so that meets the rules. The pages do not say whether simply replacing the paid model with a different free model is enough. Do not claim that the replacement repeats the private model's results without measuring it. Saved outputs alone also do not prove that the whole process can be repeated. If an essential private component cannot be used to repeat the work under these conditions, ask the organizers before making it part of the final design.

## Ownership wording

The challenge rules say that the team and organizing committee jointly own submitted materials. They also protect the rights of other resource owners and require separate written consent for the listed commercial uses. Read the full terms; this guide does not settle what they mean legally.

Wording that is still unclear: the team-formation deadline, a bonus that refers to a missing judging area, and some differences between the overview and rule sections. See [open questions](15_open_questions.md).
